# Product

## Register

product

## Scope

This context describes the design diff viewer, not the terminal or marketing site.

## Users

Developers and designers reviewing their rendered code against Paper while working in fx. They need to inspect small visual differences without interrupting their work.

## Product Purpose

Show source, Paper, and their visual differences with a direct route into synchronized zoom and pan. Success is an inspectable comparison, not an AI-written style audit.

## Brand Personality

Direct, restrained, polished. The user prefers less text, flat colors, rounded corners, and intuitive states.

## Anti-references

Verbose reports, walls of token warnings, decorative gradients, and technical status strings presented as primary UI.

## Design Principles

- Put visual evidence before explanation.
- Keep diagnostics available without making them the default experience.
- State what is known; time passing alone does not prove a comparison stale.
- Preserve direct keyboard, wheel, pinch, and pan interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, status announcements, and reduced-motion support. Label comparison directions with words and symbols as well as red and green.
